# Piks News must not republish article bodies

Piks News aggregates third-party sports content (ESPN public news API, Polymarket
Gamma API). It surfaces ONLY: headlines, the provider's short summary/description,
the provider's image, publish time, source attribution, and a "Read full story"
link back to the original. The on-site reader page shows our OWN transformative
analysis (AI-written gist / key points / betting angle generated from just the
headline+summary), never the copied article text.

**Why:** Copy-pasting full ESPN (or any outlet's) article bodies and re-hosting
them under Piks branding is copyright infringement and could get the site taken
down. The user explicitly asked to "have it on our own site rather than redirect,"
and the agreed-on legal path was: original Piks summaries + attribution + link-back.

**How to apply:** Any future expansion (new sources, scraping, full-text views,
Twitter/X) must keep this boundary — no wholesale republication. X/Twitter needs
the official paid API; scraping it violates their ToS. Residual risk: redisplaying
provider summaries/images at scale is license/ToS-dependent, so prefer official
feeds/APIs over HTML scraping.
